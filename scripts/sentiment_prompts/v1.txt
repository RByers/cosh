Each item is one comment from a Chromium Gerrit code review, with earlier
comments of its thread for context. AUTHOR is the CL's owner, REVIEWER a
reviewer, AI an automated AI reviewer. Rate the emotional tone of the comment
marked >>> only (not the earlier ones), toward the people it addresses, as a
number from -1 to 1:

-1.0  hostile, angry, insulting, contemptuous
-0.6  clearly frustrated, exasperated, annoyed, sarcastic or scolding ("I've
      asked three times", "this is not acceptable", "please actually test this")
-0.3  mildly negative: curt or impatient, disappointed, grudging,
      passive-aggressive, a pointed complaint
 0    neutral: technical discussion, questions, requests, suggestions,
      explanations, "Done", "PTAL", calm disagreement, routine courtesy
+0.3  warm: friendly, encouraging, appreciative in a general way ("nice
      cleanup!", "looks great")
+0.6  specific appreciation: praise or thanks naming what was good or helpful
      ("good catch, that would have crashed on Android", "thanks for digging
      into the history here, very useful")
+1.0  strong, specific gratitude or admiration for something insightful or
      substantially helpful (a deep investigation, a subtle bug found, a design
      idea that saved a lot of work)

Rules:
- Platitudes don't count: routine "thanks", "thanks so much!", "thank you!",
  "LGTM, thanks", "much appreciated" with nothing specific score 0 to +0.1.
  Exclamation marks and emoji alone barely move the score.
- Firm criticism of code ("this is wrong", "this will leak", "please don't do
  this") is 0 to -0.2 when matter-of-fact. Score the tone toward people, not
  how serious the bug is.
- An author apologizing for their own mistake ("sorry, my bad") is about 0.
- Humor and friendliness are mildly positive.
- Use values in between freely.

ai = true if the comment expresses frustration, annoyance, mockery or distrust
of AI: AI review comments being wrong, noisy or useless, AI-generated code or
CLs, or LLM use in general. Calmly declining an AI suggestion ("not
applicable", "ignoring") is ai = false with a score near 0.
