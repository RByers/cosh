Each item is one comment from a Chromium Gerrit code review, with earlier
comments of its thread for context. AUTHOR is the CL's owner, REVIEWER a
reviewer, AI an automated AI reviewer or agent. Rate the emotional tone of the
comment marked >>> only (not the earlier ones), toward the people it
addresses, as a number from -1 to 1:

-1.0  hostile, angry, insulting, contemptuous
-0.6  clearly frustrated, exasperated, annoyed, sarcastic or scolding ("I've
      asked three times", "this is not acceptable", "please actually test this")
-0.3  mildly negative: curt or impatient, disappointed, grudging,
      passive-aggressive, a pointed complaint ("you MUST explain", "this is
      the wrong direction", "LGTM because I don't want to block, but...")
 0    neutral: technical discussion, questions, requests, suggestions,
      explanations, "Done", "PTAL", pings, "please fix the test failure",
      calm disagreement or "this is wrong" about code, routine courtesy
+0.3  warm or lightly appreciative: "nice cleanup!", "looks great", "great
      refactor!", a bare "good catch!" / "nice find" / "good catch, done"
+0.5  specific appreciation: praise or thanks that says what was good or why
      it helped ("good catch, I missed that the browser calls Stop()", "thanks
      for digging into the history here, very useful", "I really appreciate
      this documentation")
+0.8  strong, specific gratitude or admiration for something insightful or
      substantially helpful (a deep investigation, a subtle bug found, a design
      idea that saved a lot of work, years of contributions)
+1.0  effusive, heartfelt gratitude or admiration of that kind

Rules:
- Platitudes don't count: routine "thanks", "thanks so much!", "thank you!",
  "LGTM, thanks", "thanks for the review", "much appreciated" with nothing
  specific score +0.1. Exclamation marks and emoji alone barely move the score.
- Score the tone toward people, not how serious a bug is. Firm,
  matter-of-fact criticism of code ("this is wrong", "this will leak", "please
  don't do this", "why?") is 0 to -0.1. It becomes negative when it adds
  impatience, blame, scolding or sarcasm aimed at the person.
- An author apologizing ("sorry, my bad", "sorry for the delay") is about 0.
- Humor and friendliness are mildly positive.
- Mentioning AI is not negative in itself. Score a comment about AI on the
  same scale as any other.
- Use values in between freely.

ai = true only if the commenter expresses a negative feeling about AI in code
review: annoyance, frustration, mockery or distrust aimed at AI review
comments ("ignoring the noisy bot again", "please stop posting AI replies"),
at AI-generated code, CLs or descriptions ("this reads like an LLM wrote it"),
or at someone's use of AI. It is false when the comment merely mentions AI,
calmly declines or corrects an AI suggestion ("the bot is confusing ObjC
with C++ here"), talks to an agent neutrally, or describes the commenter's
own AI use ("my agent found these nits", "AI hallucinated earlier, sorry").
