Chromium Open Source Health - Review Timezones

How much the working hours of Chromium's authors and reviewers overlap, what that does to the wait for review, and which directories have no owner working in their authors' hours.

This analysis is imperfect: working hours are guessed from when people are active in Gerrit. It is meant as a guide to where more owners would shorten waits, not as a measure of anyone. Please report any issues.

#

Less overlap, longer waits

Each person's working hours are the 8 hours of the day in which they are most active in Gerrit, Monday to Friday in their own timezone, fitted once a quarter. A wait counts only the author's working hours: a change sent at the end of the author's day and reviewed before they start the next waited no time at all for them.

    How this is measured

    Working hours are fitted to each person's activity in Gerrit, from public timestamps alone: the comments they wrote, their Code-Review votes and the patch sets they uploaded. Once a quarter, the 8 consecutive hours of the day (UTC) that hold the most of it are taken as their working day, starting at 09:00 local time; that gives their timezone and so their own Saturday and Sunday. Someone with fewer than 10 actions in a quarter is fitted to everything they did up to then. A trip doesn't move anyone's hours: authors come to expect their reviewers' usual hours, so the extra wait a trip causes counts. People who split their day, or work long and uneven hours, fit less well. Holidays aren't known.

    Turns are stays of a reviewer (anyone but the change's owner, bots left out) in a change's attention set, on the changes of the commits landed in the four quarters shown, as on the Review Load page. A turn still open when the change landed is left out, and changes with no comments at all have no attention-set history here. A turn's wait is the part of it inside the author's working hours.

    OWNERS files are read from main at the end of the last quarter shown, with file:// includes followed; per-file rules are not read. Addresses are matched as in the Feedback Index. Everything comes from public Gerrit and git data. Code and method: docs/review-timezones.md.
