# AI markers, adoption by directory, and footers (draft, 2026-11-08)

Draft analyses for the balance page (`docs/balance.md`), from
`scripts/balance_ai.py`. Everything is offline: the shared Chromium clone
and `data/private/balance*.sqlite`. Commit messages are author or bot text,
so they are only matched with regexes, never interpreted. All figures here
are aggregates; none is per person. **The directory analysis is
speculative.**

## Regenerating

From the worktree or main checkout, with the report inputs from
`docs/balance.md` (`first_commit.txt`, `owners_added.txt`, the narrow
`ai_marked.txt` and `mech.jsonl`):

```
export COSH_DATA_DIR=<main>/data/private
python3 scripts/balance_ai.py markers [--sample 40]   # -> $COSH_DATA_DIR/ai_marked_broad.txt
python3 scripts/balance_ai.py paired  --first-commit F --owners-added O --ai-marked A --mech M
python3 scripts/balance_ai.py did     --first-commit F --owners-added O --ai-marked A --mech M
python3 scripts/balance_ai.py footers --first-commit F --owners-added O --ai-marked A --mech M
```

`markers` takes about a minute. Each of the others loads every CL and commit,
which takes 3–5 min and several GB of RAM, so run them one at a time.
`footers` streams nine years of commit messages. The person-level results use
the fixed identity merging in `balance_growth.alias_pairs` (2026-11-08), and
were rerun with it. Tests: `python3 -m unittest tests.test_balance_ai`.

## 1. The broad AI marker

`ai_marked_broad.txt` has one line per commit: sha, change number, rule. It
covers first-parent commits on `main` since 2024, with reverts and relands
left out. The rules, in order:

| Rule | What | Commits |
|---|---|---|
| campaign | one bulk dead-TODO removal ("This is an AI-generated dead TODOs removal.") | 491 |
| agent | `Agent-Generated: true` trailer | 609 |
| trailer | `Co-Authored-By`/`Assisted-by`/`AI-Model` naming an AI tool, in the last paragraph only | 244 |
| phrase | the text says the CL was made with an AI tool ("Generated with gemini-cli", "This CL is AI generated", `#ai-assisted`, "refined with gemini-cli", "written by Codex", …) | 1,183 |
| **all** | | **2,527** |

That is 4× the trailer-only set (620), and it starts in mid-2025 rather than
July 2026. By month: 30 (2025-07), 127 (2025-08), 345 (2025-11, mostly the
campaign), 140 (2026-01), 36 (2026-06), 251 (2026-09), 578 (2026-10).

How false positives are avoided:
- **Chrome features:** bare "Gemini", "AI" or "agent" count only when the line
  opens by saying this CL or its code was made with them. So "[AIM]",
  "[AutofillAI]", "Ask Gemini" and "summaries generated by Gemini" don't count.
- **Other tool mentions:** commits that add `GEMINI.md` or `.claude/` files,
  or that change the Gemini CLI tooling itself, don't count.
- **Quoted lines:** lines quoted with `>`, as in reverts and relands, are
  skipped.
- **Imports:** co-author trailers count only in the last paragraph, so an
  upstream log inside an import doesn't count.

**Quality.** Two random samples of 40 matches, each read as the matched line
only, were 80 of 80 genuine AI-assisted CLs. Two samples of 40 near misses
(lines with AI words that matched no rule) held 10, then about 4, true
markers. The rules were widened after each sample, and the second sample's
misses ("vibe-coded with gemini-cli", "AI assisted with fixing…", "[Agent
fix]") are now caught too. So recall is roughly 90% of what the messages say.
Most AI use carries no marker at all.

**Who it covers** (merged, human-owned, non-mechanical CLs since mid-2023):

| Rule | CLs | Authors | Top-5 share | Top-1 share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| agent | 557 | 3 | 100% | 72% |
| trailer | 243 | 47 | 65% | 29% |
| phrase | 1,140 | 113 | 52% | 21% |
| campaign | 491 | 5 | 100% | 39% |
| all | 2,431 | 161 | 50% | 17% |

The `Agent-Generated` trailer, the page's only marker so far, comes almost
entirely from three people. The broad set reaches 161 authors, but half its
CLs still come from five. Any "AI CL" comparison describes a few heavy users.

## 2. Same author, same quarter

The paired means of differences, marked minus unmarked, over author-quarters
with both kinds of CL:

| Measure | Narrow (trailer list), n=62 | Broad, n=201 | Broad without campaign, n=200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| log(1+lines) | +0.24 [−0.19, +0.67] | **+0.49 [+0.28, +0.69]** | **+0.49 [+0.28, +0.70]** |
| threads | −0.52 [−1.84, +0.81] | +0.48 [−0.28, +1.23] | +0.47 [−0.29, +1.23] |
| threads per 100 lines | −0.25 [−2.44, +1.94] | +0.13 [−1.01, +1.28] | +0.04 [−1.09, +1.17] |
| load (threads ÷ expected) | −0.14 [−0.48, +0.21] | +0.13 [−0.10, +0.36] | +0.11 [−0.11, +0.34] |
| interdiff share | +0.00 [−0.14, +0.14] | −0.04 [−0.10, +0.03] | −0.04 [−0.11, +0.03] |
| rounds | −0.07 [−0.40, +0.26] | +0.06 [−0.14, +0.25] | +0.05 [−0.14, +0.24] |
| log(1+h to first review) | −0.52 [−1.01, −0.03] (n=47) | +0.12 [−0.15, +0.39] (n=144) | +0.07 [−0.20, +0.35] |
| reverted < 30 d | +0.00 [−0.03, +0.04] | −0.01 [−0.02, +0.01] | −0.01 [−0.02, +0.01] |

- **The only clear difference is size:** marked CLs are about 1.6× as large
  (e^0.49) as the same author's other CLs that quarter.
- **Review is the same:** threads, load, rework, rounds and reverts don't
  differ.
- **The page's faster-first-review finding doesn't replicate.** The narrow
  set's "first review sooner" (about 34 h, log −0.52, n=47) is gone in the
  broad set, which has three times as many author-quarters (+0.12, interval
  includes 0). It was most likely an artefact of three trailer users.

**Event study by person.** For the 161 authors who ever marked a CL, this
looks at their *unmarked* CLs from 4 quarters before to 4 after their first
marked CL. Each value is relative to never-marked authors of the same tenure
band that quarter, averaged per person first, so the ± is across people.
Nothing moves after the first mark:
- size: +0.07 before against −0.02 to +0.06 after;
- load: +0.01 to +0.13 before, +0.02 to +0.19 after, all within about ±0.2;
- log hours to first review: −0.14 to +0.29 throughout, each ±0.17;
- reverts: within ±0.01.

Starting to mark AI use doesn't change the rest of someone's work in any
measure we have. These authors were already slightly larger-CL, higher-load
people before their first mark.

## 3. Adoption by directory (speculative)

The analysis covers 115 three-level directories with ≥ 400 CLs since mid-2023.

**Treatment A, agent-instruction files.** The first `AGENTS.md`,
`GEMINI.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `SKILL.md`, `.gemini/`, `.claude/` or `.agents/`
added in the directory, below it, or in an ancestor below the root. Root
files apply to everything and are left out.

**Treatment B, usage.** The first quarter in which ≥ 5% of the directory's
CLs, out of at least 20, carry the broad marker.

**Does a file predict use? No; this supports the user's doubt.**
- Only 15 busy directories have a file. Only 5 of them ever reach the usage
  threshold, while 36 reach it without a file.
- Where both happen, usage came first in 3, in the same quarter in 1, and
  file first in 1.
- After their file, the 15 file directories averaged 1.4% marked CLs per
  quarter (2025 Q3 on), against 2.1% for directories that never got one.

**Event studies** (−4..+4 quarters, against not-yet-treated and
never-treated directories that quarter, normalised to quarter −1, ± 1.96 SE
across directories):
- **File treatment:** flat everywhere. With 15 directories, and 2–5 by
  quarter +3, the intervals are wide.
- **Usage treatment (41 directories):**
  - Pre-trends are flat: load −0.01 to −0.04, threads per 100 lines
    −0.02 to −0.22.
  - Afterwards, load drifts to −0.08 to −0.10 (±0.14–0.17), threads per 100
    lines to −0.40 to −0.49 at +1..+2 (±0.7), and interdiff to −0.03 to −0.05
    (±0.05–0.06).
  - Hours to first review, rounds, reverts and newcomer share show nothing.
  
  That is the direction of "thinner review once AI is in use", but no cell
  is distinguishable from zero.

## 4. Other footers

Share of human commits with each footer, Q3 of each year:

| | 2019 | 2021 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 Q3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Owners-Override | 0% | 3.3% | 2.2% | 2.0% | 3.0% | 2.4% |
| Auto-Submit | 20.9% | 26.2% | 26.9% | 27.8% | 30.2% | 24.7% |
| Low-Coverage-Reason | 0 | 0 | 1.5% | 2.5% | 0.3% | 0.3% |
| No-Try | 0.55% | 0.25% | 0.10% | 0.09% | 0.11% | 0.14% |
| Disable-Rts | 0 | 0 | 0.04% | 0.29% | 0.33% | 0.13% |
| Validate-Test-Flakiness | 0 | 0 | 0.25% | 0.12% | 0.29% | 0.10% |
| Bug/Fixed | 81% | 85% | 87% | 88% | 88% | 90% |

Each footer's CLs compared with the same author's other CLs that quarter:
- **Owners-Override:** these CLs are much smaller (log lines −1.05) and get
  less review (load −0.34). The rate is flat at 2–3% since 2021, with no
  AI-era change. It doesn't track reviewer scarcity: across 441
  directory-years, the correlation with commits per approver is r = −0.08.
- **Auto-Submit:** smaller CLs (−0.96), slightly less review (−0.15) and
  fewer reverts (−1.6 points). Flat at 25–30% since 2021, a little lower in
  2026.
- **Low-Coverage-Reason:** bigger CLs (+1.2) with more review (+0.41). It
  fell from 2.5% to 0.3% between 2024 Q3 and 2025 Q3, before most AI use: a
  tooling or policy change, not a behaviour signal.
- **No-Try (781 CLs) and Disable-Rts (706):** reverted much more often
  (+11.5 and +5.1 points). Real, but tiny, and about CI shortcuts rather than
  review balance.
- **Validate-Test-Flakiness:** test changes; no review difference.

## Verdicts

- **Broad marker: use it, with care.** Precision is 80 of 80 on hand checks,
  4× the trailer set, and starts in 2025. Half its CLs come from five
  people, so treat it as "what heavy AI users do", not "what AI does".
- **Same-author effect:** marked CLs are about 1.6× larger, with no
  measurable difference in review, rework or reverts. The page's "first
  review sooner" claim should be withdrawn: it doesn't hold on the larger
  set.
- **Adoption files: weak signal, as the user suspected.** They are rare in
  busy directories (15 of 115) and don't predict marked use: file
  directories use AI *less* than the rest.
- **Usage-based directory DiD: suggestive, not conclusive.** After a
  directory starts using AI visibly, review per CL drifts down by about 0.1
  load, with flat pre-trends. No cell is significant with 41 directories.
  Worth repeating as the broad marker accumulates quarters.
- **Footers: not relevant to the balance goals.** Owners-Override and
  Auto-Submit are flat since 2021, unrelated to reviewer load, and mark
  small, routine CLs. Low-Coverage-Reason's drop is a tooling change.
  No-Try's revert excess is a small CI-quality aside.

## Caveats

- Marking is voluntary and team-dependent until 2026-07, and most AI use is
  unmarked. So unmarked CLs and never-marked directories are contaminated
  controls, and every effect here is biased toward zero.
- The person-level analyses depend on identity merging. The 2026-11-08 fix
  (`alias_pairs`) changed the paired counts, from 52 to 62 author-quarters for
  the narrow set.
- Hours to first review are clock hours.
